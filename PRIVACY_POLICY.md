# Privacy Policy for ZNeko Launcher

**Last Updated:** July 24, 2026

**ZNeko Launcher** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ZNeko Launcher handles information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and Process

ZNeko Launcher is designed as a local-first frontend and organizational utility. We do **not** run tracking services, analytics suites, or advertising SDKs.

#### A. Local Storage Access
- **Functionality**: ZNeko Launcher requests access to user-selected folders via Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) to scan, index, and organize game files and custom artwork stored on your device.
- **Data Handling**: All folder indexing, configuration settings, and artwork caches are processed and stored strictly on your local device. We do not upload your personal files or ROM contents to external servers.

#### B. Local Network Communication (ZNeko Link)
- **Functionality**: ZNeko Launcher includes a local device transfer feature (ZNeko Link) that operates over your local Wi-Fi network (LAN).
- **Data Handling**: File transfers occur directly between your devices on your local network. No transfer data or network traffic is routed through external cloud servers.

#### C. Optional Third-Party Services
If you choose to use optional online features, ZNeko Launcher communicates directly via encrypted HTTPS connections with the following third-party services:

- **ScreenScraper.fr**: If you connect your ScreenScraper account, your username, password, and game filenames are transmitted to ScreenScraper.fr to retrieve retro gaming artwork and metadata.
- **SteamGridDB**: If you provide a SteamGridDB API key, search queries and asset requests are transmitted to SteamGridDB.com to fetch artwork.
- **RetroAchievements**: If you log into RetroAchievements, your credentials and game hashes are sent to RetroAchievements.org to display achievement progress.

Each third-party service processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

---

### 2. Data Security

All sensitive credentials (such as ScreenScraper logins, SteamGridDB API keys, and RetroAchievements credentials) are saved locally on your device and transmitted exclusively over secure HTTPS protocols to their respective services.

---

### 3. Children's Privacy

ZNeko Launcher does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted by updating the **Last Updated** date at the top of this document.
