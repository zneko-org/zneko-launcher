TL;DR: Upload square art to SteamGridDB for at least 5 supported platforms (either as Icons or as Grids). ZNeko will automatically group them under your name as an installable pack!

# Making Folder Icon Packs for ZNeko

ZNeko's **Assets Catalog** lets you easily restyle the icons of your platform folders. Every pack in it comes from [SteamGridDB](https://www.steamgriddb.com). ZNeko does not host any artwork of its own. It reads what people upload to SteamGridDB and groups it into packs.

---

## How ZNeko builds a pack

ZNeko asks SteamGridDB for the icons and grids of every platform in the [list below](#supported-platforms) and groups them by uploader. A pack is one person's artwork for many platforms.

| Rule | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Square, static images | **Icons** can be any square size. **Grids** must be exactly **512 × 512** or **1024 × 1024**. Animated uploads are not used. |
| At least 5 platforms | An uploader needs square art for **5 or more** of the supported platforms to become a pack. |
| Icons and grids are separate packs | Your icons form one pack and your grids form another. Each one needs its own 5 platforms. |
| Newest upload wins | If you uploaded more than one icon for the same platform, ZNeko uses the most recent one. |
| Grids are not mostly transparent | For grid packs, ZNeko checks the first grid, following the order of the list below. If more than half of it is transparent, the pack is treated as a set of borders or overlays and left out. Icons are not checked, since icons are expected to have transparent backgrounds. |

The catalog on each device refreshes once a week, or right away when the user presses **Refresh** on the Assets Catalog home screen.

Search in the Assets Catalog looks for the **uploader's name** among the packs that follow these rules.

## It takes some patience

SteamGridDB stores artwork per game, and it lists each platform as a game of its own. On the website, that means opening each platform's page and uploading its icon one by one. A whole set can also be uploaded through SteamGridDB's API, but that takes some technical setup.

It's worth it, though: packs with more folder icon coverage appear first in ZNeko.
